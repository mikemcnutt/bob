Assessment Security

Cheating

Using an electronic device or other materials without permission during an exam will be considered cheating regardless of intent.

Other forms of cheating include, but are not limited to:

  • Having information available in any non-approved form, including papers, books, or notes.
  • Using materials hidden in apparel or written on oneself or a desk.
  • Tabbing out to another computer screen during an assessment.
  • Looking at another student’s work.
  • Communicating with other students in any manner during an assessment.

Document Integrity

Plagiarism

At MCTC, plagiarism is a serious academic offense that can result in a student failing an assignment, failing a course, or being dismissed from the college. Students are expected to exhibit honesty in all work. Students caught plagiarizing or cheating will be subject to the disciplinary policy stated in the KCTCS Student Code of Conduct.

Additional information about avoiding plagiarism is available in the MCTC Student Guide: Plagiarism.
